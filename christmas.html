Christmas

Our Christmas celebrations began in 2023 as well!

We've taken turns hosting Christmas across 3 different households so far. We usually do some sort of gift exchange: White Elephant, Secret Santa, etc.. We feast in a way that's similar to Friendsgiving, and always make sure to make some good hot cocoa. What has become one of my favorite parts of our Christmases is picking a theme for outfits, like pj's, and colors!
